Insurers have got smarter — and they reward customers who actively reduce the risk of big water-damage claims. For a property portfolio, the premium discount can easily cover the whole investment in sensors, before you even count the value of the damage you avoid.

## The three things the insurer typically wants to see

1. **A sensor plan** — where the sensors are placed and what they cover.
2. **Response time** — how quickly an alarm reaches the on-call person.
3. **Event log** — an audit trail with test alarms and real events.

All of this is produced automatically by the platform, so you don't have to compile it manually.

## What happens when things do go wrong?

Water detection **isn't** about avoiding every claim — some leaks are unavoidable. But the time stamps from the sensors make a huge difference when the claim is assessed:

- **When did the leak start?** — the sensor has the first detection.
- **When did someone act?** — the escalation log shows who got the alarm and when they acknowledged.
- **How big is the damage?** — the sensor shows how long water has been running.

That means **faster claim settlement**, less debate about the deductible and typically a smaller claim overall — because action was taken quickly.

## Typical business case

For a housing association with 120 flats and a shared basement that we've worked with:

- **Investment**: around DKK 30,000 for 40 sensors and the platform over 3 years.
- **Premium discount**: 12 % of the water-damage insurance — about DKK 18,000 per year.
- **Avoided damage, year one**: a detected leak under a washing machine on the first floor, stopped after 4 minutes. The loss adjuster assessed the avoided damage at DKK 180,000.

On top of that comes the peace of mind of knowing that no leak is allowed to develop unnoticed for weeks.
