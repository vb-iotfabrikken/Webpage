A water-damage claim rarely starts with a bang. It starts as a drop per second behind a pipe casing — and is only discovered when water is standing in the basement or the paint starts to bubble on the wall upstairs. By that point you are no longer talking about a repair, but a renovation.

That's why water detection isn't about **whether** water shows up, but about **how quickly you find out**.

## From drop to alarm in under 60 seconds

RoomAlyzer's water detectors are small, wireless units that sit low on the floor or are fixed in wet zones. The moment water touches the sensor:

1. The sensor sends a message into the platform.
2. The platform matches the alarm against your escalation rules.
3. The on-call person gets SMS and email — and a backup person is contacted if no one acknowledges.

The whole sequence typically takes under a minute, whether it's 2 p.m. on a Tuesday or 3 a.m. on Christmas Eve.

## Where should we put them?

Typical placements at our customers:

- Under washing machines, dishwashers and fridges with water connections.
- By [water heaters and plant equipment](/en/articles/leak-sensors-for-basements-and-plant-rooms/) in basements.
- At pipe penetrations between floors.
- Under raised floors in [server rooms and data centres](/en/articles/water-alarms-in-server-rooms/).
- In museum stores, archives and depots, where a leak can ruin irreplaceable assets.
- In empty holiday homes and second residences without regular supervision.
