Poor indoor climate is one of the biggest invisible drags on schools. When CO₂ rises above 1,200 ppm, students' ability to focus drops noticeably — and above 2,000 ppm, both headaches and fatigue start to set in. At the same time, CO₂ is one of the easiest parameters to measure, understand and act on.

## Measure every classroom — not just the building

With a single RoomAlyzer sensor per room you get a **precise, continuous picture** of air quality. Caretakers and management get:

- Alarms when CO₂ rises above your chosen thresholds during teaching hours.
- Monthly reports per classroom, per floor and per school.
- Comparisons across buildings in the municipality.
- Documentation that holds up to workplace inspections or questions from the student council.

## From measurement to action

Data alone doesn't change the air. That's why RoomAlyzer can pass CO₂ data on to your ventilation system via [MQTT](/en/articles/mqtt-integration/), so ventilation ramps up precisely where it's needed — and back down when the room is empty.

This gives you the best of both worlds: better indoor climate for students **and** lower energy consumption.

## Typical setup for a school

1. One MINI+ sensor per classroom (up to 10 years of battery life).
2. One Full sensor in a few reference rooms (also gives VOC and sound levels).
3. A single gateway per building — or pure NB-IoT if the municipality prefers it.
4. Platform access for caretakers, management and, where relevant, a facilities advisor.

We handle the whole setup for you and help you set the thresholds, so you don't drown in alarms.
