If you work with building preservation in churches — or are part of the daily operations — you know how important it is to protect these buildings and their furnishings. Churches are not just rooms for faith and tradition. They are often filled with priceless cultural heritage: historic organs, wall paintings, wood carvings, antique furniture and manuscripts that must be preserved for future generations.

Many of these materials are especially sensitive to changes in humidity. **Too much moisture** can lead to mould, rot and corrosion — especially on organ pipes, altarpieces and pews. **Air that is too dry** can cause cracks in wood, dry out adhesives and damage old documents. The damage often occurs slowly and invisibly, but by the time it's discovered it is expensive and time-consuming to repair — and in the worst case irreversible.

That is why it is essential to be able to follow humidity levels **continuously** and act in time.

## Get a full overview — locally and centrally

As a verger, priest, advisor or buildings officer in a diocese, you carry the responsibility for good conditions in the church's rooms and buildings. With RoomAlyzer you can monitor humidity in every part of the church — from the sacristy to the organ loft, the roof space and the crypt.

You get:

- Warnings in time if the climate moves outside your desired limits.
- Clear graphs and reports that document the measurements over time.
- A single view across multiple churches if you sit at the diocese.
- App access for vergers and priests — act from your phone.

It makes it easy to prioritise inspections, maintenance and interventions **before damage occurs**.

## Easy installation — flexible solutions with long battery life

**RoomAlyzer MINI+** is our most popular sensor for churches and historic buildings. It measures both temperature and relative humidity with high accuracy and includes an algorithm that automatically assesses the risk of mould growth.

- Sends readings about every two hours over the mobile network (NB-IoT).
- Up to 10 years of battery life.
- Requires no WiFi, cabling or gateway.
- Installed discreetly with a small screw or an adhesive pad — and can be moved as needed.

If you need near-real-time data in particularly sensitive rooms, MINI+ can be supplemented with **RoomAlyzer HUMIDITY**. The combination gives you both broad coverage and fast reaction times.

You get intuitive graphs, user-friendly reports and notifications via SMS or email when something requires action. We take care of the whole setup — both hardware and software — and help you get started smoothly.
