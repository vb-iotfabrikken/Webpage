This is the IoT Fabrikken blog. It's where we publish longer reads — the kind
that don't fit in a library article because they mix opinion, customer
stories, product news and the occasional deep dive into a standard or a piece
of research.

## What lives here

- **Company news.** Funding, partnerships, certifications, hiring.
- **Product milestones.** Major releases, new hardware, notable platform
  changes.
- **Opinion pieces.** Our take on PropTech, sensor hardware, indoor climate
  and the state of the industry.
- **Longer customer stories.** The ones that need space to breathe and
  include numbers you can cite.

## What lives in the library instead

- **Use cases and how-tos.** Structured articles with a fixed anatomy
  (eyebrow, hero, key points, FAQ). They live in `/en/library/` and are
  indexed across all 19 catalogs.
- **Integration docs.** Step-by-step walkthroughs for connecting RoomAlyzer
  to the rest of your stack.
- **Glossary and FAQ.** The permanent reference material.

If you're new here, start with the [library](/en/library/). If you want to
follow the company, subscribe to the RSS feed or come back here every few
weeks.
